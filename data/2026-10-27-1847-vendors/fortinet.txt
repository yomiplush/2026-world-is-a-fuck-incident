We strongly urge every customer to plan and promptly complete these upgrades as soon as the releases are available, prioritizing internet-facing devices. These releases include fixes to address vulnerabilities identified through our frontier AI-assisted security testing program . Fortinet would like to thank Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, and SpaceX for their collaboration throughout this process. What makes this release different Fortinet has integrated frontier AI models into our product security testing process. These models analyze code paths, protocol handling, and configuration logic at a depth and speed that traditional fuzzing and manual review cannot match. This work has helped surface vulnerabilities more efficiently than conventional methods, allowing us to proactively fix them before they may be found and exploited by malicious actors. This is our secure-by-design commitment in practice: finding and closing weaknesses at the source. We are also being transparent about one implication. The same class of AI capability is becoming available to attackers, particularly as open weight models improve. The window between disclosure and exploitation continues to shrink, and we expect it to keep shrinking. Why you should upgrade immediately We recommend customers treat this release as a priority change rather than a routine maintenance cycle. Four factors drive this urgency: Criticality of fixes: These releases include critical and high-severity fixes. Faster weaponization: AI tools can now help adversaries reverse-engineer patches and build working exploits in hours or days, rather than weeks. Patch diffing risk: Once updates are public, the fixes themselves reveal where changes were made, exposing a potential system weakness. Unpatched devices then become easier targets for attackers. Chaining of vulnerabilities: AI has the ability to link seemingly unrelated lower severity vulnerabilities together to achieve a more severe, exploitable vulnerability. Our recommendation is to apply temporary mitigations, including limiting exposure by managing devices out of band, moving resources behind VPN or SASE where feasible, and applying virtual patches until the patching can be prioritized. What to do, starting today Prepare today so you can upgrade as soon as these releases are published on October 22. Inventory: Identify every FortiManager device and FortiOS instance, prioritizing those that are exposed to the internet Schedule maintenance windows: Prioritize change windows for October 22–23 for internet-facing systems Back up configurations: Save current configurations before upgrading Monitor: Watch logs for unusual administrative access or unapproved configuration changes before and after patching If you cannot upgrade immediately, reduce exposure in the interim by: Restricting administrative interfaces (HTTPS, SSH) to trusted IP addresses or disable them on external interfaces Ensuring FortiManager administration is not reachable from the internet Following best practices, which include: Using strong unique credentials Enforcing MFA for all administrator accounts These steps reduce risk but do not replace the need for a timely upgrade. Our commitment We believe customers are best protected when vendors proactively find and fix vulnerabilities and disclose them responsibly. Using frontier AI in our own testing is another way we stay ahead of attackers using the same tools. We will continue to apply these methods across the Fortinet portfolio and share our findings with customers in the spirit of security transparency. Thank you for acting quickly. For upgrade assistance, contact Fortinet Technical Support or your account team.
