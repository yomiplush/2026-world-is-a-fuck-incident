# Hacker Initiative 2026: unresolved application questions

Reviewed: **2026-10-30**. Applicant context: independent developer in Japan; prefers payment without a paper check. This is a preparation checklist, not an eligibility determination. No contact, form submission, or payment arrangement has been made.

Status meanings: **Verified rule** means expressly published; **Not found** means absent from the official materials reviewed, not prohibited; **Clarification needed** means the applicant or funder must resolve it before relying on the draft.

## Eligibility and project scope

- **Verified rule:** Individuals and independent security/privacy researchers are listed applicant categories. **Clarification needed:** Does HI accept this particular Japan-resident individual and project? Category eligibility alone does not establish geographic eligibility. [Grant guidelines](https://hackerinitiative.org/grant-guidelines/)
- **Verified rule:** The homepage describes worldwide support; 2026 emphasizes Signal/Noise, including source/provenance tools. **Clarification needed:** Can the actual work demonstrate a technical benefit to the hacker community? Worldwide mission language does not confirm this applicant's acceptance. [2026 announcement](https://hackerinitiative.org/)
- **Verified rule:** Website/media production, general awareness/advocacy, salaries/staff training, and tuition are excluded. **Clarification needed:** Is the proposed funded work principally an excluded website/news briefing or a distinct eligible technical project? Which concrete expenses are allowable? Do not change labels to disguise the work. [Application process and exclusions](https://hackerinitiative.org/apply-now/)
- **Clarification needed:** How does HI treat applicant compensation, contractor research fees, and genuine third-party services? A salary exclusion does not by itself settle every contractor arrangement, and calling developer compensation a service does not establish eligibility. Obtain a ruling before treating such costs as fundable. [Grant guidelines](https://hackerinitiative.org/grant-guidelines/)
