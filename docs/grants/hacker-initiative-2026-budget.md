## Documented assumptions and missing inputs

1. The initial technical scope is two existing evidence families, one offline verifier, a schema contract, fixtures and repository instructions. It does not require a new website or continuous collection infrastructure.
2. The inspected verifier and evidence tests use Python's standard library. The website's development dependency is not evidence of a paid requirement for this toolkit. Local execution in the preparation environment is not proof of the applicant's equipment ownership, cloud credits or future resource availability.
3. No hardware quantities, paid CI minutes, storage volumes, subscription periods, third-party rates or work hours have been authorized by the maintainer or substantiated by a quote for this proposal.
4. No outside sponsor, co-funder, matching contribution or volunteer labor is committed in this draft. A public GitHub Sponsors link does not prove income or a grant-payment arrangement.
5. USD would be the budget currency. No JPY conversion rate, tax treatment, fee allowance or unrestricted reserve is assumed.
6. The proposed development window in the application is a planning scenario. It is not an estimate of billable hours or a promise that the applicant can supply unpaid labor.

## How a defensible budget could be completed

First identify a real need that is necessary for the bounded technical deliverables. Then determine whether the foundation permits that exact expenditure. Only after that should the maintainer obtain a quotation and approve the quantity and timing.

For each accepted line, retain: item/vendor; technical purpose; cost classification; quantity and unit price; currency and dated conversion basis if applicable; taxes/fees treatment; quotation date; expected payment milestone; other funding; and the foundation's written clarification where needed. Do not count the same cost or labor twice. Record in-kind contributions separately only after the contributor confirms them; do not use a fabricated in-kind valuation to justify a cash request.

A valid sum is the total of necessary, evidenced, permitted cash line items. If that sum is below USD 5,000, do not pad it with unnecessary equipment, a general reserve, or relabeled compensation. Ask whether the opportunity can accommodate the genuine scope; otherwise use another funding route or revise the funding plan for substantive reasons.

## Payment and cash-flow dependencies

The payment inquiry is a prerequisite, not a settled administrative detail. The [questions document](hacker-initiative-2026-questions.md#payment-and-tax-questions) records the published check method and the absence of confirmed SWIFT, PayPal or GitHub Sponsors recipient options. The applicant prefers not to receive a paper check.

The generic [reporting terms](https://hackerinitiative.org/grant-reporting-template/) describe two equal installments, with later payment tied to reporting. Before committing expenses, obtain the actual agreement and determine payment timing, permitted pre-award spending, reduced-award treatment and who carries any interim cash requirement. Do not assume the applicant can advance purchases or that an unspecified alternative payment will be approved.
