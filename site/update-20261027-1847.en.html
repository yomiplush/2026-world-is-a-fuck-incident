Fireblocks mpc-lib: ancestor shares of 1 party differ from a complete private key

CVE-2026-90975 involves fallback to zero when a parent secret is absent during BIP32 hardened derivation. An attacker obtaining a descendant signer share and chain code may be able to reach an ancestor/root share of the same MPC party. The threshold layer remains, and this is not described as independently yielding a spendable private key. The CERT text says no fix is available.

What to checkProtect chain codes as secrets and do not distribute them through watch-only extended public keys or similar means. Do not treat hardened derivation as a guarantee of isolation between shares; if a leaf share is compromised, investigate that party's entire hierarchy. Do not use unconfirmed fixed-version numbers to make deployment decisions.
