Ongoing response priorities

1 · Public exposure and active exploitation

Update and investigate compromise in parallel.

PHP, GitLab, Arista, CODE, Plex, SiteGuard, and others. Check public reachability, affected configurations, and running versions. Installing a patch does not prove an existing intrusion has been removed.

2 · Keys and cloud permissions

Keys can remain exposed after recovery.

LiteLLM, Railway, ConnectWise, and others. Preserve logs and check leaked keys, added owners, permissions, and consent. For keys used outside your app, consult provider-side logs.

3 · Bundled components and appliances

Verify what is actually running.

Includes .NET self-contained applications, Brotli, JRE, certifi, kernels, and appliances including PAN-OS. A source fix or downloaded update alone does not complete remediation.

4 · Research and uncertainties

Narrow the conditions required for exploitation.

Check Tailworm, AI/MCP, and other issues by permissions, features, and input paths. Preserve unconfirmed minimum fixed versions and numbering discrepancies, and reduce unnecessary permissions and exposure.
