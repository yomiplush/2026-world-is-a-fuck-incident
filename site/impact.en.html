Actual harm and business disruption confirmed

Business, medical and service disruption · Source updated: 2026-10-27

IDCF Cloud: disruption and encryption affecting 495 companies and municipalities.

Confirmed impact
VM shutdown and encryption across 4 zones in East Japan Region 1. Backup retrieval confirmed possible for 206 customers. Unauthorized queries affected portal registration information for up to 71 companies, with up to 4 unauthorized password changes.
What remains unknown
206 is not the number of completed recoveries. The company says data inside VMs was not exfiltrated and traces of registration-information exfiltration remain unconfirmed. Final recovery dates for all customers and total losses are undisclosed.
Recovery and user actions to check
Reset passwords. Support alternative-environment migration and backup retrieval.

Primary sources: IDCF第7報・10月27日

Confirmed financial impact · Source updated: 2026-10-14

infoQ: exfiltration of 89 ten-thousand and 4,082 records and unauthorized point exchanges

Confirmed impact
Exfiltration of 894,082 personal information records confirmed. 611 unauthorized exchanges totaling 2,869,500 yen. Service suspended since 10/3, also affecting some commissioned surveys.
What remains unknown
The initial maximum of 948,498 records is not a confirmed damage count. The vulnerability CVE, final recovery date, and total completed compensation remain unconfirmed in the text.
Recovery and user actions to check
All member passwords invalidated. Policy is to fully reimburse fraudulently exchanged points. Change passwords on other services where the same password is used.

Onamae.com: business environment shutdown and data exfiltration

Confirmed impact
Service suspended since 10/21. External exfiltration of customer information confirmed. Perpetrator site domain suspension confirmed around 04:00 on 10/28.

Business, medical and service disruption · Source updated: 2026-10-28

@link: patient booking and related service suspension

Confirmed impact
Suspended since late night on 10/14. Database information acquisition and personal information leakage from late September (month 9) to mid-October (month 10) were confirmed. Patient apps, web reservations, and other functions remained suspended as of the 28th at 12:30.
What remains unknown
Leakage counts and the resumption date for all functions remain under investigation and verification. Starting beta testing at some medical institutions is not resumption of general availability.
Recovery and user actions to check
Block intrusion paths and disable related credentials. Contact medical institutions directly to change reservations. Set a new password upon resumption.

Confirmed impact
Malicious 1.107.0 and containers were actually distributed on 10/21, confirming compromise of release infrastructure.
