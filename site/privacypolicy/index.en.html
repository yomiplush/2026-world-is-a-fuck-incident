Privacy policy

Effective: 10/25, 2026 · Last updated: 10/27, 2026 · Statement specific to this site.

Optional storage: no choice made (disabled)

The scope is www.infosec-briefing.net briefings and related pages. You can view reports, sources, and motion graphics even if you decline optional storage. The operator is YomiPlush（yomiplush）, and the contact address is contact@yomiplush.dev .

1. Information handled by this site and its purposes

  • Consent and rejection choices:Your choices, selection time, expiration, and consent-explanation version are stored in this browser. This storage is necessary to avoid repeatedly asking the same choices and to control optional storage. The app does not send it to a server.
  • Display language:Only with consent, remember the language to display it again next time. You can still change the language immediately if you decline. Languages specified in URLs and browsing history are subject to normal browser management.
  • Delivery and security:We use Cloudflare to deliver pages, images, and same-site audio. In doing so, Cloudflare may process IP addresses, access dates and times, requested URLs, HTTP headers, connection/device information, and other data. The purposes are preventing unauthorized access, maintaining connections, and delivering content.
  • Display and interaction:The app consults browser language, reduced motion and data saver settings, available rendering performance, and similar information within the browser to adjust display load and behavior. It does not send these to a separate analytics service. Audio plays after user interaction.
  • Contact:Email addresses, message bodies, and other information provided by email are used to reply and handle requests. Please do not send sensitive information unnecessary for your inquiry.

This site's app has no analytics tags, advertising pixels, advertising cookies, account registration, personalized advertising, profiling, or automated legal decision-making. Choosing consent does not provide blanket permission for future analytics or advertising.

3. Giving, refusing and withdrawing consent

In the initial choice panel, you can choose Reject optional storage or Consent to saving settings. Optional language storage is not performed before consent, without a selection, or after expiry. Continuing to browse, scrolling, using the unlock button, or operating audio alone is not treated as consent.

You can change your choice through “Cookie settings” at the bottom of any page or the settings button on this page. Changing consent to rejection deletes the saved language. Withdrawal does not retroactively invalidate lawful processing before withdrawal. If your browser prohibits storage, the choice applies only while that page is open. Choices are managed separately for each browser, device, and site origin.

4. Processing grounds, recipients, and processing abroad

Where GDPR applies, optional language storage relies on consent. Necessary delivery, security, and inquiry handling serve the legitimate interests of providing a secure, available information site and responding to requests. We comply with legal obligations to retain or disclose information when applicable. Rules on device storage also take applicable regional laws into account.

Cloudflare handles information related to delivery and protection. Its services may process information abroad, including in the United States. We do not promise that all processing is restricted to the EU. Its role, explanations and safeguards concerning transfers, and retention criteria are Cloudflare privacy policyfor details.

Retention periods for delivery and defense logs depend on the service, settings, purpose, and legal obligations; no uniform number of days is specified here. This app has no independent visitor history database. Inquiry information is retained as long as needed to respond and, afterward, to the extent necessary for disputes or legal requirements. Contact the operator about specific processing or retention.

5. External links and your rights

GitHub, Patch the Planet, sources, and GDPR references are external links. We do not embed third-party social posts or ads; when you follow a link, that provider's policies apply. Sending an inquiry email also involves your email service.

Depending on applicable law, you may access, correct, or delete information; restrict processing; exercise data portability; object to processing based on legitimate interests; withdraw consent; or complain to a supervisory authority. Browser choices and display language can be deleted directly from your browser. Requests concerning server-side processing should be sent to contact@yomiplush.dev please send to. We verify request details and identity to the extent necessary.
