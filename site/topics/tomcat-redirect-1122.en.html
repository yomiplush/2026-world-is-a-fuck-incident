Apache Tomcat: redirect handling update

Apache disclosed an issue in redirect handling for deployments that use RedirectValve. The Apache release guidance was cross-checked with the NVD record.

What to check

For affected deployments, follow Apache guidance and update to a supported version at or above 11.0.29, 10.1.62, or 9.0.124 for your branch. Verify redirect and access-control configuration.
