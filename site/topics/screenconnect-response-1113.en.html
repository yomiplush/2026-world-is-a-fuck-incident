ScreenConnect: separate technician-client protection from compromise remediation

ConnectWise’s 11/12 18:45 UTC update identifies approximately 400 environments with RMM+ extensions for precautionary action, not confirmed compromises. Its current assessment finds no evidence of compromised ConnectWise infrastructure or a ScreenConnect vulnerability in initial access. Clients at 26.6.5 or later block the SCKit endpoint-to-technician spread technique, but do not remove existing compromises.

What to check

Verify the installed client on every technician workstation is 26.6.5 or later; keep the identified extensions disabled. Revoke web/IdP sessions from a trusted device and investigate with independent EDR or other out-of-band tooling. Preserve evidence, then clear queued commands/Toolbox items, unauthorized clients and persistence; validate before reconnecting.
