DrayTek Vigor2960 and D-Link routers: Web management CGI buffer overflows, command injection, and unpatched EoL device risks

In DrayTek Vigor2960 firmware 1.5.1.6 and earlier, 5 vulnerabilities in `/cgi-bin/mainfunction.cgi` were published: 4 heap or stack buffer overflows via `keyPath` in `login` (CVE-2026-129449), `rtick` in `sub_1E084` (CVE-2026-129450), `file` in `app_guest_export` (CVE-2026-129451), and `upload_name` in `check_file_exist` (CVE-2026-129453), plus OS command injection via configuration field manipulation in `set_table` (CVE-2026-129452). The NVD records note vendor guidance to update to 1.5.1.7 alongside plans for a further fix release. Additionally, legacy D-Link router issues were published or revised: `authentication.cgi` and HNAP stack overflows in DIR-600L 2.17 / 2.17b02 (CVE-2026-129353, CVE-2026-129354), default FTP credentials in DSL-6740C (CVE-2026-129444), hardcoded password in DIR-850L (CVE-2026-129445), and HNAP overflows in DIR-878 1.20B05 marked End-of-Life [EoL] in the modification window (CVE-2026-124560, CVE-2026-124561).

What to check

Update DrayTek Vigor2960 to 1.5.1.7 or later and block WAN exposure of the web management interface (`/cgi-bin/mainfunction.cgi`) pending the additional planned release. For End-of-Life (EoL) or unpatched D-Link models (DIR-600L, DIR-850L, DIR-878, DSL-6740C), block internet-facing management access and replace with supported hardware.

A first fixed version is listed only when the advisory provides one. Advisory counts are not incident counts.
