NATS Server: check MQTT exposure and fixed releases

The NATS Server advisory reports pre-authentication memory exhaustion on the MQTT listener. Check where MQTT is enabled and which networks can reach it.

What to check

Upgrade to 2.12.12 or 2.14.3 or later for the applicable release branch. Restrict MQTT listeners to necessary networks and verify connectivity and memory use after rollout.

A first fixed version is listed only when the advisory provides one. Advisory counts are not incident counts.
