Apache Shiro: Any-password acceptance when DefaultLdapRealm caching is enabled, plus 8 annotation and session advisories

On 11/14, 8 Apache Shiro advisories were disclosed on oss-security. In configurations up to 3.0.0 that enable non-default `authenticationCachingEnabled=true` on DefaultLdapRealm (or JndiLdapRealm), cached authentication after 1 successful login skips the LDAP bind and the default `AllowAllCredentialsMatcher` accepts any password (CVE-2026-129349, CVSS 4.0 9.1). Also disclosed: session fixation on RememberMe restoration and acceptance of URL session IDs when URL rewriting is disabled (CVE-2026-74762, through 3.0.0); unenforced interface/superclass authorization annotations in `shiro-guice`, `shiro-aspectj`, `shiro-cdi` and `shiro-jakarta-ee` (CVE-2026-74763, through 3.0.1); cookie attribute stripping and untrusted host replay during saved-form resubmission in `shiro-jakarta-ee` (CVE-2026-88019, 2.0.0-alpha-0 through 3.0.1); case-sensitive static-file authentication bypass on manually created `ShiroFilterFactoryBean` in `shiro-spring` 3.0.1 (CVE-2026-114737, incomplete fix for CVE-2026-23903); empty stored credentials matching empty submitted passwords (CVE-2026-129347, through 3.0.1); deleted users and narrowed permissions remaining active after `PropertiesRealm` reload (CVE-2026-129348, through 3.0.1); and unescaped `shiro:principal` tag XSS (CVE-2026-129320, through 3.0.1).

What to check

Upgrade Apache Shiro to 3.0.2 or later (CVE-2026-129349 and CVE-2026-74762 are fixed in 3.0.1, and the remaining 6 advisories are fixed in 3.0.2). If immediate upgrade is not possible, disable LDAP authentication caching (the default) or configure a credentials matcher such as `SimpleCredentialsMatcher`, place authorization annotations directly on implementing methods, and call `setCaseInsensitive(true)` on manually created `ShiroFilterFactoryBean` instances.

A first fixed version is listed only when the advisory provides one. Advisory counts are not incident counts.
