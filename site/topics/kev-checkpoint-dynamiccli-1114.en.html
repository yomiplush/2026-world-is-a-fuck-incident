Check Point DynamicCLI: added to KEV, root privilege escalation via 0777 local socket

CISA added CVE-2026-123039 to KEV on 11/13 (due 11/16). On Gaia OS, the root `dynamic_server` process listens on `/tmp/dynamic_socket` with world-writable (0777) permissions and no peer authentication, allowing an attacker with existing local execution to run arbitrary commands as root. Check Point observed active exploitation against a couple of dozen customers. Spark Firewalls and Smart-1 Cloud are unaffected.

What to check

Apply the Jumbo Hotfix Accumulator (R82.20 Take 14, R82.10 Take 52, R82 Take 132, or R81.20 Take 174 and later) and verify `ls -l /tmp/dynamic_socket` shows `srw-rw---- root config (0660)` rather than `0777`. Stopping `dynamic_server` via `dbset process:dynamic_server` and `dbset :save` mitigates the socket exposure but breaks sk144112 Dynamic CLI commands. Audit `/var/log/dynamicCli.log` for `Received =` lines missing the Clish wrapper and for `Rejected connection from unauthorized uid`.
