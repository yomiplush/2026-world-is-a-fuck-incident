PRIMARY-SOURCE REVIEW · 2026-11-17T09:00:00+09:00

qbee-agent 2026.41 Zip Slip, Directus 12.3.1 share privilege escalation, and termux-sync 1.2.4 across 3 issues.

In qbee-agent prior to 2026.41, app/utils/tar.go unpackTar checks paths with strings.HasPrefix without boundary slashes, enabling Zip Slip outside context directories during Docker Compose extraction (CVE-2026-129237). In Directus prior to 12.3.1, non-admins with directus_shares update permission can modify user_created and role to administrator IDs, deriving admin permissions for guest share links (CVE-2026-129239). termux-sync prior to 1.2.4 fixes arbitrary file writes via manifest path traversal (CVE-2026-86046).

What to check

Upgrade qbee-agent to 2026.41 or later, Directus to 12.3.1 or later, and termux-sync to 1.2.4 or later. Audit share update logs in Directus where directus_shares permissions were delegated to non-administrators.
