create-ecdh, Kroki, Frappe india-payroll and angular-editor: Unvalidated elliptic-curve public keys and SVG / editor XSS

In `create-ecdh` 4.0.4 and below, the JavaScript implementation (`browser.js`, used by browser bundlers) does not validate that peer public keys lie on the curve or in its prime-order subgroup, enabling invalid-curve or small-subgroup private-key recovery when a static private key is reused, or predictable shared secrets on low-order points (CVE-2026-129319; modern Node.js native re-export is unaffected; fixed in 4.0.5). Also published: reflected XSS via `javascript:` links in DBML table attributes on Kroki's `/dbml/svg` endpoint before 0.36.0 (CVE-2026-129447, fixed in 0.36.0), missing authorization in `tax_regime_selector.py` in Frappe `india-payroll` before 16.0.4 allowing low-privileged users to read other employees' pay details or alter draft tax regimes (CVE-2026-129429, fixed in 16.0.4), and 2 XSS issues bypassing `DomSanitizer` in `@kolkov/angular-editor` (CVE-2025-71465 fixed in 3.0.3; CVE-2025-71464 fixed in 3.0.4).

What to check

Upgrade `create-ecdh` in browser bundles to 4.0.5 or later, Kroki to 0.36.0 or later, Frappe `india-payroll` to 16.0.4 or later, and `@kolkov/angular-editor` to 3.0.4 or later.
