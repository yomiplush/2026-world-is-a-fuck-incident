CloudPaste: public share links can expose WebDAV credentials

The CNA record for CVE-2026-129494 covers CloudPaste 1.1.0–1.9.1. An unauthenticated user knowing a public share slug may obtain WebDAV Basic Authorization from /api/proxy/link responses and then read, modify or delete files on the backing storage. A fixed release number was not confirmed in the reviewed material.

What to check

Restrict public-sharing and WebDAV integration routes, and revoke and replace storage credentials. Removing a shared link alone is insufficient; review WebDAV access logs and the credentials' permission scope.
