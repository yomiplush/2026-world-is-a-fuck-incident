libp2p: Fixes for gossipsub topic map exhaustion DoS and ASN.1 DER decoder stall vulnerabilities.

In libp2p (js-libp2p), 2 severe DoS vulnerabilities have been fixed. In @libp2p/gossipsub, receiving subscription RPC messages without default topic limits or per-peer caps allows remote peers to grow a node's topics map without bound, causing memory exhaustion and DoS (CVE-2026-119793). In @libp2p/crypto, during Identify message public key ASN.1 DER decoding, an attacker-controlled length field triggers an unbounded synchronous loop, stalling the Node.js event loop for 4 to 5 seconds per connection and transiently allocating 2 GB of memory (CVE-2026-110789). These were fixed in gossipsub 17.0.0 and crypto 5.1.22 respectively.

What to check

Upgrade @libp2p/gossipsub to 17.0.0 or later and @libp2p/crypto to 5.1.22 or later. On public nodes, configure maximum topic subscription limits and verify pre-authentication input length validation.
