SimpleSAMLphp SAML2: Authentication bypass via HTTP-Artifact signature validation omission (incomplete fix of CVE-2026-49283).

In simplesamlphp/saml2, a vulnerability (CVE-2026-110782 / GHSA-r7hw-jx6r-756g) was identified stemming from an incomplete fix for CVE-2026-49283 (HTTP-Artifact TLS validator confusion allowing cross-IdP authentication bypass). Although HTTPArtifact::receive() verifies embedded SAML Response signatures, it lacked binding between the embedded Response's Issuer and the HTTP-Artifact resolution entity ID, creating risk that unsigned embedded Responses or assertions from third-party IdPs would be accepted. Fixed in simplesamlphp/saml2 6.2.4 and 7.0.0-rc2.

What to check

Upgrade simplesamlphp/saml2 to 6.2.4 or later (or 7.0.0-rc2 or later). In SP environments using HTTP-Artifact binding, verify IdP signature validation and Issuer consistency.
