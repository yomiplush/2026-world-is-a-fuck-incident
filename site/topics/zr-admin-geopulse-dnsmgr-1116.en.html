Zr.Admin.NET, GeoPulse, dnsmgr, Course-tencent-cloud, and Magma: 12 IDOR and authentication/authorization flaws in enterprise and cloud management systems

In the enterprise management platform Zr.Admin.NET (up to commit `eb9228a`), 5 vulnerabilities were published (upstream Issue #27, no fixed release version listed): `/common/DownloadFile` `path` parameter traversal reading `JwtSettings:SecretKey` from `appsettings` to forge administrator tokens (CVE-2026-129464), administrator account takeover by tampering with `UserId` in `SysProfileController.UpdateProfile` combined with `/checkMobile` and `/PhoneLogin` (CVE-2026-129465), and IDOR / missing authorization on workflow applications, private articles, and scheduled job SQL definitions (CVE-2026-129454, CVE-2026-129455, CVE-2026-129466). Also published were sequential ID enumeration on `GET /api/v1/places/geocoding/{id}` exposing other users' location history in GeoPulse 1.5.0 through 2.3.0 (CVE-2026-129467, commit `b994ec58` / PR #673), missing authorization on `POST /cloudflare/get_domain_default_line` in Netcccyun `dnsmgr` 2.18 to prior to 2.22 (CVE-2026-129468, fixed in 2.22 and commit `175468c6`), refund preview IDOR via default Sqids key in `Course-tencent-cloud` prior to 2.0.2 (CVE-2026-129469, fixed in 2.0.2), and 4 flaws in Linux Foundation Magma 1.9.0 and earlier involving `X-Magma-Client-Cert-Serial` header spoofing, hardcoded credentials, and RBAC/XSS (CVE-2026-129357 to CVE-2026-129360, vendor unresponsive and no fix available).

What to check

Update dnsmgr to 2.22 or later, Course-tencent-cloud to 2.0.2 or later, and apply commit `b994ec58` to GeoPulse. Since no official fixed release is available at capture time for Zr.Admin.NET or Magma 1.9.0 and earlier, remove public exposure, strip client certificate headers (`X-Magma-Client-Cert-Serial`) at the reverse proxy, and rotate/protect JWT secret keys.

A first fixed version is listed only when the advisory provides one. Advisory counts are not incident counts.
