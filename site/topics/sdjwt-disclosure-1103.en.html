SD-JWT: silently discarded disclosures can change authorization

CVE-2026-62374 silently discards mismatched Disclosures and returns success. A valid signature can accompany fewer claims, affecting verifiers that allow access when a constraining claim is absent. Required key binding rejects on-path tampering; tampering by the holder remains a distinct condition.

What to check

@sd-jwt/core through 0.19.0 is affected, with 0.20.0 listed as the first fix. @sd-jwt/decode through 0.19.0 is affected but has no first fix listed. Reject missing required claims and verify each deployed dependency separately.
