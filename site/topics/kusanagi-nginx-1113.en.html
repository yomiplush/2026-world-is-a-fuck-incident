KUSANAGI: the 11/12 revision clarifies Nginx redirect scope

JVN published on 11/09 and revised affected scope on 11/12. CRLF handling in WordPress Nginx redirect rules can inject response headers when a crafted URL is visited. Affected: all KUSANAGI 7; all 8 Free/Business/Premium; 9 Security/Enterprise through 10.7.2-1 and Free/Business/Premium through 9.12.2-1. Apache httpd configurations are excluded.

What to check

Check the edition and Nginx configuration, then apply the developer’s latest update or official workaround. Keep Apache exclusions explicit and do not infer a minimum fixed version absent from JVN.

A first fixed version is listed only when the advisory provides one. Advisory counts are not incident counts.
