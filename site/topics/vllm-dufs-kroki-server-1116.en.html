vLLM, dufs, and Kroki: KV cache common prefix race condition, WebDAV COPY overwrite authorization flaw, BlockDiag SSRF, and Goat SVG XSS

A race condition in `single_type_kv_cache_manager.py` (Common Prefix Handler) in vLLM 0.30.0 and 0.31.0 (CVE-2026-129356, fixed in 0.32.0 and commit `e687a251`) and an authorization flaw in the WebDAV `COPY` handler (`src/server.rs`) of dufs 0.46.0 and earlier that allowed overwriting existing files without checking delete permission (CVE-2026-129355, fixed in 0.47.0 and commit `fba13dff`) were disclosed. For Kroki prior to 0.36.0, in addition to the 4 PlantUML/Graphviz issues recorded previously, 2 more vulnerabilities fixed in 0.36.0 were published: unauthenticated SSRF and local image file read via node `icon` and `background` attributes in BlockDiag engines (CVE-2026-129485, GHSA-jwh8-j584-79hp) and reflected XSS via unvalidated `svg-color-dark-scheme` / `svg-color-light-scheme` options on `GET /goat/svg` (CVE-2026-129484, GHSA-xp94-px2v-f7f2).

What to check

Update vLLM to 0.32.0 or later, dufs to 0.47.0 or later, and Kroki to 0.36.0 or later. In dufs deployments exposing or sharing WebDAV, check whether accounts lacking delete permission have overwritten existing files.
