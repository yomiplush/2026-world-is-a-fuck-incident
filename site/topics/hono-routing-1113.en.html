Hono: mount and router path differences can bypass authentication middleware

Mount-prefix removal, re-normalization and RegExpRouter wildcard matching may reach inner handlers without the outer authentication middleware. Separate text/ping CSRF, multipart form validation and css-helper advisories retain their version details below.

What to check

Update hono to 4.13.16. Authenticate inside the mounted application; consider the documented TrieRouter workaround where needed. Regenerate PreparedRegExpRouter data and check custom patterns now rejected by the fixed router.

A first fixed version is listed only when the advisory provides one. Advisory counts are not incident counts.
