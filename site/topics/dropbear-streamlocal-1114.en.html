Dropbear SSH: authorized_keys permitopen/permitlisten restrictions are not enforced on Unix-socket forwarding

Disclosed on oss-security on 11/13 (no CVE assigned; verified at commit 99baf182). While `permitopen="host:port"` and `permitlisten="host:port"` in `authorized_keys` are enforced on TCP forwarding (`svr-tcpfwd.c`), the default-enabled Unix domain socket forwarding channels (`direct-streamlocal@openssh.com` and `streamlocal-forward@openssh.com` in `svr-streamfwd.c`) check only the coarse `no-port-forwarding` flag and never consult per-destination restrictions. An authenticated caller holding a destination-restricted CI or backup key whose OS user can access `/var/run/docker.sock` or database sockets can reach them over streamlocal. This is not unauthenticated.

What to check

No upstream fixed release is available yet. Do not rely on `permitopen` or `permitlisten` alone to isolate accounts from local Unix sockets; add `no-port-forwarding` where forwarding is unneeded or remove restricted-key accounts from the `docker` group and sensitive socket permissions.
