SvelteKit: 4 advisories for adapter route-config injection and Node server crashes

4 SvelteKit advisories: an unhandled URIError crash when `reroute` rewrites malformed percent-encoded paths (CVE-2026-128117 in `@sveltejs/kit`), prerendered paths interpreted as regular expressions in generated Vercel routing configs (CVE-2026-128121 in `adapter-vercel` and `adapter-static`), arbitrary redirect rule injection into Cloudflare `_redirects` during prerendering (CVE-2026-128122 in `adapter-cloudflare`), and an `adapter-node` crash when mutating headers on immutable requests proxied via `fetch` (CVE-2026-128123).

What to check

Update the packages and branches in use—`@sveltejs/kit` to 2.70.4 / 3.1.1, `@sveltejs/adapter-vercel` to 6.3.5 / 7.0.2, `@sveltejs/adapter-static` to 3.0.11 / 4.0.1, `@sveltejs/adapter-cloudflare` to 7.2.10 / 8.0.2, and `@sveltejs/adapter-node` to 5.5.8 / 6.0.1—then rebuild and redeploy.

A first fixed version is listed only when the advisory provides one. Advisory counts are not incident counts.
